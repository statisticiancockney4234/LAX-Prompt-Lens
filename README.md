## 🧐 What Is LAX-Prompt-Lens?

Imagine you are browsing your favorite anime artwork site, and you spot a gorgeous illustration. You love the style,the colors,the character design. ## 🎯 How It Works (Without the Technical Mumbo-Jumbo)

Ever wonder how LAX-Prompt-Lens is so good at turning tags into prompts? Here is a behind-the-scenes peek:



### 🏷️ Tag Extraction:The Digital Librarian

Danbooru has millions of images,every single one carefully labeled by a community of volunteers. When you look at an image,those labels,hundreds of them,are hidden underneath the surface. LAX-Prompt-Lens reads those labels in real time. Think of it like having a librarian whisper every detail of a painting into your ear—colors,poses,expressions,clothing,accessories,background elements,lighting mood,composition style—everything.





### 🧠 Prompt Generation:The Art Director

Once the tags are extracted,LAX-Prompt-Lens doesn't just dump them in a jumbled mess. Oh no,that would be chaos. Instead,it uses a clever algorithm that groups related tags together and organizes them in a logical,professional sequence. Here's what the generated prompt looks like:

```
Masterpiece anime illustration, [character name] with [hair color] hair, [eye color] eyes, wearing [clothing description], standing in [background setting], cinematic lighting, dynamic angle, detailed background, highly detailed, sharp focus, [mood adjective] atmosphere, anime key visual style, high quality, 4k, trending on ArtStation
```

See how it flows? It starts without setting the scene,then adding details,and finally specifying quality enhancers. This structure is exactly what top AI art models respond best to,giving you consistent,beautiful results every time.



### 🎛️ Customization Options

You are not locked into one style. Click the LAX-Prompt-Lens icon,and then click the gear icon to open **Options**. Here you can:
- 🎚️ **Adjust creativity** —Control how "wild" the prompt variations can get. Lower values stay faithful to the original; higher values introduce more artistic liberty.
- 🎨 **Choose art style bias** —Prefer prompts that lean toward "photorealistic," "watercolor," "3D render," or "anime classic" styles.

- ✂️ **Toggle tag categories** —Decide whether to include character tags,copyright tags,or general tags in your prompt. Perfect for when you want only the character withoutthe series background.
